Proxying domain

Pixel, clicks and unsubscribe go through this domain. If it gets blocked, enter a new one: only new emails change, old links keep working through any domain that proxies to the panel. Empty: the panel address.

Link signing secret

Tracking and unsubscribe links are signed with a secret. Rotate it if it may have leaked. Links in mail already sent keep working until the next rotation: the previous secret is still accepted, the one before it is dropped. Click and open links expire after 180 days, unsubscribe links after 2 years.
